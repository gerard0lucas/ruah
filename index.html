Before the first call.

What does Ruah actually take on?

+

Websites, software, workflow automations, Google Ads and Meta Ads, AI agents, custom LLM and RAG systems, analytics and conversion work, and e-commerce. Most engagements combine two or three of these, because they depend on each other.

Can you work with what we already have?

+

Yes. A site that already ranks, an ad account with history, a stack of tools, a store that is live. We start from the constraint in front of you and keep what is working.

How does a project start?

+

A short brief, then a call. You leave with a written scope: what we will build, the boundary around it, how long the first release takes, and how we will know it worked.

What is inside a custom LLM or RAG engagement?

+

We structure your source material, retrieve the relevant parts when a question arrives, evaluate answer quality, and place the system where your team or customers already work. Answers can cite their sources. Sensitive actions stay behind an approval.

What do we receive at launch?

+

The repository, the accounts, and documentation. If you want us to keep operating ads, experiments, or agents after launch, that is a retainer. It is a choice, separate from the build.

Where do you work?

+

With teams remotely. The work lives in writing, on calls, and in regular releases you can use.
